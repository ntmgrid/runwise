This Privacy Policy explains how Runwise ("Runwise", "we", "us"), operated by [Registered legal name] of [Registered address, Karnataka, India], collects and uses your personal data when you visit our website or use our WhatsApp-based service. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made under them.

1. Who is the Data Fiduciary

For the data described here, Runwise is the "Data Fiduciary". Contact: ntmgrid@gmail.com, +91 97784 54998.

2. What we collect

  • Sign-up details: name, company name, email address, WhatsApp/phone number, chosen plan and billing preference.
  • Service content: messages, voice notes (and their transcripts), tasks and calendar entries you send us on WhatsApp.
  • Connected-tool data (only if you connect them, on plans that include them): data from accounting, payment, billing, invoicing, CRM, spreadsheet and calendar tools you authorise us to read, used to produce reminders, runway estimates, summaries and investor updates.
  • Payment details: handled by our payment provider [Payment gateway name]. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (IP address, browser type, time of request) for security and troubleshooting.

3. Why we use it (purposes)

  • To create and run your account and deliver the Runwise features you chose.
  • To send reminders, summaries and, if you instruct us, updates to people you nominate (such as your CA, CS or investors).
  • To process payments, keep tax and accounting records and meet legal obligations.
  • To respond to your requests and provide support.
  • To send product news only if you opted in. You can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep our service secure and prevent abuse.

We collect only the data necessary for these purposes. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Your consent

We process your data based on your consent given when you submit our form or start using the service, and for certain legitimate uses permitted by law (for example, complying with legal obligations). You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us; withdrawal does not affect processing done before it, and may mean we can no longer provide some features.
