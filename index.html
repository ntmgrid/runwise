FAQ

Fair questions.

Is Runwise a replacement for my CA or CS?

No. Runwise is a reminder and organisation assistant. It is not a chartered accountant, company secretary, lawyer or financial adviser and does not give professional advice. Due dates can change by notification, so always confirm filings with your professional.

How accurate is the runway prediction?

It is an estimate based on the data you connect. Quality depends on how complete and up to date that data is. Treat it as a planning aid, not a guarantee or financial advice.

What data do you collect and who sees it?

Only what's needed to run the service: your contact details, the chats you send us and the data from tools you choose to connect. We don't sell your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Do I need a new app?

No. Runwise works inside WhatsApp. Calendar and other tools connect through their standard sign-in using their standard sign-in, so we do not need your passwords.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Plans renew automatically until cancelled. Details are in the Terms and Refund Policy.

Who can sign up?

Runwise is built for business owners and founders aged 18 or over.
